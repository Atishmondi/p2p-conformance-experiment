# Council verdict and disposition — P2P completion

Date: 2026-10-26. Five independent advisors and five new anonymous peer reviewers completed. Peer labels were shuffled: A Expansionist, B Contrarian, C Executor, D Outsider, E First Principles. All five reviewers selected C as strongest and A as most vulnerable to premature polish.

## Where the Council Agrees
Correct measurement and reproducibility precede presentation. Preserve the historical baseline. Distinguish synthetic examples, observational BPI proxies, and actual ERP evidence. Replace hardcoded figures and unsupported claims. Keep live ERP execution separate from the portable demo.

## Where the Council Clashes
The Expansionist proposes an impact calculator and a richer consulting engagement. The Contrarian prioritizes semantics and scope. Resolution: a short investigation/business-case document with explicit assumptions is worthwhile; a calculator and SaaS are excluded until there is defensible operating data.

## Blind Spots the Council Caught
Peer review identified policy authority, per-rule field applicability, grain/unit/currency reconciliation, incomplete cases at an observation cutoff, independently labeled holdouts, and distribution rights. Fixture correctness alone cannot establish operational accuracy; CI alone cannot prove comprehension.

## The Recommendation
Finish a bounded, explicitly fictional purchasing-policy demonstration: one comparable item/unit per case, recorded events with nonnegative delta quantities, five explainable rules, tri-state verdicts, deterministic artifact generation, portable demo, tests, and honest ERP adapter. Unsupported returns, mixed-grain data, and ambiguous ordering are rejected or abstained from as documented. Keep observed field proxies separate from these lab rules.

## The One Thing to Do First
Pin independent failing examples for quantities, incomplete evidence, and ordering, then fix the checker against them.

## Disposition
- Adopted: contract, unknown outcomes, baseline preservation, dynamic evidence/provenance, isolated runs, fresh-clone verification, short front door and business case.
- Adopted: identify policy as fictional lab policy without practitioner endorsement; exceptions are investigation leads, not accusations or monetary losses.
- Adopted: include per-rule field observable attributes, applicable denominators and proxy gaps in claim/evidence documentation.
- Adopted: incomplete cases never counted as proven compliant; clearly state observation-window and no operational precision/recall claim.
- Bounded: independent unit/integration fixtures and example walkthrough cover the supported model; an independently adjudicated operational holdout remains unperformed, explicitly disclosed rather than simulated into a field claim.
- Bounded: preserve existing public data provenance, do not redistribute the raw XES in delivery or include private source paths; avoid asserting a license not verified.
- Deferred: impact calculator, universal ERP adapter, multi-currency amount matching, returns and full payment allocation, new promotional video; unsupported cases rejected/documented.
- Runtime: actual Docker/Odoo execution requires a functioning authorized local runtime; attempt results recorded separately. An ERP-shaped CSV example is labeled synthetic.
